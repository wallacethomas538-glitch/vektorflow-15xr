🔑 How to Get Your AI API Key

Follow these steps to get a free API key for your chosen AI provider.

Groq

FREE
  1. Go to console.groq.com
  2. Click "Sign Up" (free, no credit card)
  3. Once logged in, click "API Keys" in the left menu
  4. Click "Create API Key"
  5. Name it VektorFlow and click "Create"
  6. ⚠️ Copy the key immediately — you won't see it again!
  7. Paste it into VektorFlow's "Groq API Key" field
Pro tip: Groq's free tier gives you ~1,500 requests per day. Enough for testing!

Mistral AI

FREE
  1. Go to console.mistral.ai
  2. Click "Sign Up" (free tier available)
  3. Navigate to "API Keys" in the left sidebar
  4. Click "Create New Key"
  5. Copy the key and paste it into VektorFlow
Free tier gives you 1 billion tokens — more than enough for full testing!

DeepSeek

FREE
  1. Go to platform.deepseek.com
  2. Sign up for a free account
  3. Navigate to "API Keys" section
  4. Generate a new key
  5. Copy and paste into VektorFlow

Google Gemini

FREE
  1. Go to aistudio.google.com
  2. Sign in with your Google account
  3. Click "Get API Key" in the left menu
  4. Click "Create API Key"
  5. Copy the key and paste it into VektorFlow
Free tier: ~100 requests per day. Good for testing.
